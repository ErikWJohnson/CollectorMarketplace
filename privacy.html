PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy at CollectorMarketplace.net

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This policy describes the service as it is currently configured. It must be reviewed and finalized by qualified counsel before live financial transactions or a public launch.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide when you create an account, build a profile, create a listing, message another collector, submit a trade or delivery request, or contact the service. This may include your username, email address, profile image, listing details, images or videos, messages, delivery address, location information you choose to provide, and transaction or delivery records.

We also collect limited technical information that is generated when you use the service, such as log data, device and browser information, IP address, pages or features used, and timestamps. We may use local browser storage to keep you signed in and preserve marketplace preferences.

2. How we use information

  • Operate, secure, and improve CollectorMarketplace.net.
  • Display listings, profiles, social features, and public marketplace content.
  • Facilitate communications, trades, purchases, delivery coordination, returns, fraud prevention, and support.
  • Calculate marketplace, delivery, and payment estimates.
  • Enforce the User Agreement and comply with applicable law.

3. What is public

Your username, profile details you choose to publish, listings, listing media, tags, comments, votes, and public marketplace activity may be visible to other visitors. Do not place private contact details, financial information, or precise home-address information in public listings, profiles, comments, or chats.

4. Payments and delivery providers

Payment and shipping features may use third-party providers, including PayPal, Shippo, and UPS. When you choose to use one of those features, information required to process the selected service may be shared with that provider under its own privacy terms. CollectorMarketplace.net does not store full payment-card numbers or PayPal passwords. Sandbox payment activity is test activity and is not a live payment service.

5. How we share information

We share information with the other party to a transaction when needed to complete a purchase, trade, delivery, return, or related communication. We may also share information with service providers that host, operate, secure, or support the service; with authorities when required by law or when we reasonably investigate fraud, abuse, or safety concerns; and as part of a business transfer where permitted by law.

6. Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Use strong, unique passwords and promptly report suspected account misuse.

7. Your choices

You can update certain profile information through your account. You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information where applicable law provides that right. Legal, safety, fraud-prevention, and transaction records may need to be retained as permitted or required by law.

8. Children

CollectorMarketplace.net is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Marketplace transactions and age-restricted content may have additional eligibility requirements.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. The updated date will change when we do. Material changes will be communicated through the service where required by law.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the support contact method published within CollectorMarketplace.net.
